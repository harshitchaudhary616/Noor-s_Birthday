Last stop

A letter from Harshit.

He wrote it himself. Open it when you are ready.

Noor,

Some people pass through your life like weather,
loud for a day, gone by evening.
You came like a lamp left on in a window,
and stayed, and kept me from leaving.

When the winter was harsh, and no one dared to stand beside me,
you opened your door and invited me in.
It was freezing cold outside, but you gave me a warm, comforting place to live.
I have never forgotten that.

You have always been there when my anxiety took over
and the stress felt too heavy to carry.
With you, I have always had a home,
no matter where you go, or who you are with.

You loved me like no one did before you.
You will always remain in my heart,
and our bond will never grow weak.
Like a fine wine, we will only grow better as we age.

On my heaviest days you never asked for a speech.
You said, "come, eat something,"
as if a warm plate could be a kind of prayer,
and somehow it always was.

You tend to your skin the way you tend to people,
slowly, every day, with patience,
sure that soft things deserve looking after.
You are soft, Noor, and I have never once seen that as weakness.
It is the strongest thing I know.

So here is my wish for your birthday:
may every year be as generous to you
as you have been to everyone around you.
May your table stay full, your laughter stay loud,
and may someone always run to the door when you come home.

Whatever age you turn today,
you are still the person who made my ordinary days feel like holidays.
I am so lucky to be your friend.
Happy birthday, my best friend.

Wherever your life takes you, and whoever you share it with,
may you always get back the love you give.
All the best for your marriage, Noor.
You will be the most beautiful bride, and I will be proud to see it.
All the best for your future.
I am always here for you, and you can always come home.

With all my love,
Harshit
